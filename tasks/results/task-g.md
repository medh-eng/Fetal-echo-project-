## D0 — Why hearts are missed

- **Resolved:** Population qualifier: the revised paragraph now begins, “A high proportion of the heart disease that could be detected before birth occurs in pregnancies with no risk factor and no extracardiac anomaly” (IS23-080). No claim that all heart disease is detectable.
- **Resolved:** Poorly obtained views and suspected abnormalities are separated: “A view that could not be obtained is an incomplete examination … A view that looks abnormal is a suspected abnormality” (IS23-056/078). The video also preserves “may be needed.”
- **Resolved:** The video separates the 3VV role in transposition and the 3VTV role in coarctation; the audit and detection-rate statements now describe their distinct tasks (IS23-054/067/089).
- **Minor remaining wording:** The checklist item “View not obtained: more time or a second examination” is more categorical than IS23-056. Prefer “When fetal lie is unfavourable, more time or a second examination **may** be needed.”
- **Source nuance:** The original ISUOG p.796 says referral should be timely **if the screen suggests an abnormality**. IS23-080 and the D0 paragraph (“quality routine screen … and timely referral”) elide that conditional. The editor should decide whether to clarify the ledger before changing the unit. This is a source-scope correction, not a new mandatory-referral rule.

**Result: partly resolved — small checklist qualifier and referral condition remain.**

## D1 — The normal heart in five views

The seven original actionable findings in Task E have been dealt with:

1. **Framing:** “This unit teaches one systematic assessment of five views” replaces a binary normal/abnormal-diagnosis claim.
2. **Size / beat 5:** “A normal heart is usually no larger than a third of the chest area”; beat 5 says “Usually no more than a third” (IS23-026).
3. **Ectopy / drill D1-Q21:** Prose specifies **often benign**, **low-risk populations**, frequency “more often than every three to five beats,” and persistence “more than 1 to 2 weeks.” Q21's answer and explanation preserve these distinctions (IS23-034/035).
4. **Final video beat:** Suspected abnormality is a fetal-echo candidate; an unobtainable plane may instead require extra time or a second examination (IS23-056/078).
5. **Beat 6:** “Septum examined from apex to crux … drop-out can mimic a defect” no longer certifies septal integrity from one view (IS23-044/045).
6. **Beat 10:** “Colour helps to show the V and can highlight reversed flow” now matches IS23-074.
7. **Fetal lie:** “more examination time or a second examination may be needed” preserves IS23-056.

**New substantive residual finding:** In “Why five views” the statement “Most detectable heart disease occurs in pregnancies with no risk factor at all. That is why the routine scan matters more than the referral list” cites IS23-080. Neither **most** nor **matters more than the referral list** follows from that claim; the latter risks de-emphasising timely referral. Replace with: “A high proportion of prenatally detectable heart disease occurs in pregnancies with no risk factors or extracardiac anomalies. Quality routine screening and timely referral for suspected abnormalities both matter.” This reflects original ISUOG p.796.

**Result: original seven findings resolved; one new unsupported overstatement requires correction. No re-sign-off was attempted.**
