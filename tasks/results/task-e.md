# Task E. Adversarial audit of seventeen fetal-cardiac units

**Scope.** I checked all 17 current unit files against the ISUOG screening and ASE fetal-echocardiography ledgers, including prose, tables, drill stems/options/feedback, checklists and video beats. Findings below use only those ledgers. This is a ledger-fidelity review, not independent verification of the extracted ASE claims against the PDF or clinical sign-off. Where the ledgers do not support a statement, I say **MISSING CLAIM**.

**Editor confirmation applied.** XA5's framing that routine screening covers five planes in every pregnancy is accepted as confirmed by Dr Kunda; it is not raised as a finding. I do flag other claims in that framing.

## 2. D1 — The normal heart in five views (revised)

Earlier findings resolved: **no D1-specific findings were present in Task B**; full audit performed here.

1. **“Why five views,” framing:** “A screening scan … needs to answer one question reliably: is this heart normal” over-compresses the screening task and could imply that the screen only makes a binary diagnosis. Recast as a course framing about systematic assessment of the five views; cite the routine-screen scope (IS23-001/018) if retaining a clinical assertion.
2. **Size, prose and video beat 5 / D1-Q09:** “A normal heart is no larger than a third of the chest area” omits the ledger’s “usually” qualifier (IS23-026). Restore “usually” and retain “area.”
3. **Rate and rhythm, prose / video beat 5 / D1-Q04 and Q21:** “Occasional ectopic beats are common and usually harmless” is stronger than “often benign,” and drops the low-risk-population qualifier attached to structural-disease risk (IS23-034). Use “often benign” and explain that the no-increased-structural-risk statement applies to low-risk populations. “Frequent” also needs the stated frequency trigger, and persistent irregularity should retain “more than one to two weeks” (IS23-035), not an imprecise “more than a week or two.”
4. **View-quality/referral, video final beat:** “If any plane fails its criteria, the fetus needs a fetal echocardiogram” conflates an abnormal finding with a technically incomplete or inadequate view. IS23-078 supports echo candidacy for suspected abnormality; IS23-056 says extra time or a second exam may be needed for unfavourable lie. Split these pathways. **MISSING CLAIM** for an automatic echo referral rule for every unvisualised/failed plane.
5. **Ventricular septum, video beat 6:** “Septum intact from apex to crux” implies certainty and a negative finding. IS23-044/045 require assessment and warn of acoustic dropout; small defects may be difficult to confirm (IS23-046). Say the septum is assessed from apex to crux and describe view adequacy; do not instruct learners to certify it intact from one view.
6. **Colour, video beat 10:** “Colour shows both [arches] flowing the same way” is not supported by the cited screening claim for colour. IS23-074 supports colour helping show the V and highlighting reversed flow in the ductus or aortic arch, not a general “same way” rule. Replace with that narrower claim.
7. **Fetal lie, “The sweep” / D1-Q08:** “take more time or bring the patient back” reads as a fixed instruction. IS23-056 says additional time or a second examination *may* be needed. Preserve “may.”
8. **Other categories:** The remaining cited plane descriptions and keyed drill answers have a single ledger-supported answer. No other safety or source-mixing finding.

## 5. D4 — The four-chamber view in depth

1. **“What the fetal echocardiogram adds,” pulmonary venous connections:** This section correctly labels the ASE scope and distinguishes the screening minimum from specialist requirements. Keep that distinction visible wherever the specialist checklist or video is excerpted; screening IS23-040 does not impose the ASE requirement for one vein on each side with 2D and colour (AS23-047/048).
2. **Ventricular septum, small defects:** The prose says most 1–2 mm defects are of limited significance and may close before birth (IS23-046/099). Preserve “most” and “may” in any reuse; do not turn this into reassurance that a suspected defect is benign.
3. **Fetal-sex wording:** none. **Drills:** no ambiguous first answer found. **Other source mixing/safety:** no additional finding.

## 6. D5 — Outflow tracts

1. **“What to establish” versus “Three findings confirm normal outflow tracts”:** the former lists four checks (connections, size, relative position, valve appearance/motion); the latter introduces three visual findings. The shift in count risks teaching the three visual findings as an exhaustive substitute for all four checks. Label them separately as “three crossing/continuity features,” while retaining the four screening checks (IS23-049/052/064/065).
2. **Table, Course row:** “Aorta: posterior, to the right” and “pulmonary artery: crosses in front … to its left” should remain explicitly relative to the ascending aorta and fetal orientation; avoid teaching the compact “posterior, to the right” cell as an absolute position. The supporting claim is IS23-065.
3. **Other categories:** Drill answers are unique by the cited claims. Fetal-sex wording: none. Echo material is explicitly separated and no screen/echo mandate is reversed. No additional safety finding.

## 7. D6 — Three-vessel and three-vessel-and-trachea views
