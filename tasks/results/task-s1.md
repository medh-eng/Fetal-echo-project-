## What the MTP Act 2021 says, for the course (from the Act's text in search results)

Not a claim yet. When harvested, cite the Act itself:
- The usual gestational limits in the Act do not apply where termination is necessitated by substantial fetal abnormalities diagnosed by a Medical Board.
- Every State or Union territory constitutes a Medical Board by notification; its members include a gynaecologist and a paediatrician (section 3(2D)).
- The Rules (2021) set out the Board's functions and a report form for termination beyond 24 weeks.

This needs Dr Kunda's or a lawyer's reading before it is taught; the course must state the law as it is in force when published.
