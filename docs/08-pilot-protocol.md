# Pilot: does the course improve detection?

Version 0.1, 15 Nov 2026. Draft for the editor-in-chief. Follows section 11 of the master plan. Nothing here is a clinical claim; it is a study design.

## Question

After two weeks with the Detect units, do learners recognise the planes and tell normal from not normal on clips more accurately, and how sure are they when they are wrong?

## Who

15 to 20 learners, as in the master plan: sonologists, obstetricians, radiologists and fellows who perform obstetric scans. Recruited by the editor-in-chief. Taking part is voluntary; results are stored under a code, not a name.

## Design

| Step | What | Time for the learner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Short form: specialty, years scanning, scans per week, any fetal echo training | 3 minutes |
| 2 | Test A on clips, before any access to the course | about 20 minutes |
| 3 | Two weeks of access to the course app | own pace |
| 4 | Test B on different clips of matched difficulty | about 20 minutes |
| 5 | Five questions on what helped and what did not | 3 minutes |

Half the learners get clip set 1 as Test A and set 2 as Test B; the other half the reverse. This removes any difference in difficulty between the two sets.

## The test

Each item shows one clip for a few seconds. The learner answers:

1. Which plane is this? (five planes, or "not a standard plane")
2. Normal or not normal?
3. If not normal, where is the problem? (short list of regions)
4. How sure are you? (three levels)

No clip in either test appears anywhere in the course drills. Answers are fixed by the editor-in-chief before the pilot starts.

## What is measured

- Plane identification: share correct.
- Normal against not normal: share of abnormal clips caught, and share of normal clips passed as normal.
- Localisation: share correct among abnormal clips caught.
- Time to answer.
- Confidence: how often the learner was "very sure" and wrong.
- From the app: which units were opened and which drills were failed most.

## Analysis

Each learner is compared with themselves (before against after). With 15 to 20 learners the result is a direction and a size, not proof. It is reported with ranges, and it is used to decide what to build next.
