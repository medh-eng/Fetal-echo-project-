### X-B Lesion atlas (fixed template per lesion) ◐

Template: definition and spectrum; embryology; findings view by view; Doppler; differentials; associations and genetics; evolution in utero; counselling points; delivery planning; postnatal pathway and outcomes; parent note.

- **Septal and atrioventricular:** VSD; AVSD; atrial septal anomalies and foramen ovale restriction.
- **Left heart:** aortic stenosis; hypoplastic left heart syndrome; mitral anomalies; coarctation; interrupted arch; Shone complex.
- **Right heart:** pulmonary stenosis; pulmonary atresia with intact septum; tricuspid atresia; Ebstein anomaly and tricuspid dysplasia.
- **Conotruncal:** tetralogy of Fallot and variants (pulmonary atresia with VSD, absent pulmonary valve); transposition; corrected transposition; double-outlet right ventricle; common arterial trunk.
- **Arch and vessels:** right arch; double arch and vascular rings; aberrant subclavian artery; ductal constriction and absence.
- **Veins:** persistent left SVC; anomalous pulmonary venous connection; interrupted IVC; ductus venosus agenesis.
- **Position and laterality:** heterotaxy (right and left isomerism); situs inversus; dextrocardia; ectopia cordis.
- **Univentricular hearts:** double-inlet ventricle and others.
- **Myocardium and pericardium:** cardiomyopathies; non-compaction; tumours; effusion.
- **Other:** aneurysms and diverticula; coronary fistulae; conjoined twins.
